Spontaneous Dialect-Aware Speech Corpus for Low-Resource Dakhini, A Southern Indo-Aryan Language: Methods, Challenges, and Insights

Dakhini is a contact variety of the Deccan region that blends Persian, Old Urdu, Kannada, Marathi and Telugu. It is widely spoken but almost absent from curated speech resources. This study examines what it takes to collect it honestly: telephonic conversations between familiar speakers, an asymmetric awareness protocol to reduce the Observer's Effect, and a rule-based annotation layer that treats mismatches between standard-Hindi ASR output and the audio as evidence of dialect rather than as transcription error.
